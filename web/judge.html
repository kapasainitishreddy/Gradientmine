The problem isn't
GPU access. It's risk.

An applied-AI team knows its model is failing a business metric. Paying for more labor or compute does not promise the metric improves. GradientMine makes the desired outcome the unit of purchase.

THE PRODUCT CATEGORY

Model bug bounties.

Define a narrow metric, invite independent fixes, verify them on a held-out set and reward an eligible outcome. This is outcome procurement, not a new token or a GPU rental market.

THE FIRST WEDGE

Start narrow. Prove one fix.

Classification and retrieval issues with usable metrics are the starting point. Enterprise-grade large-model bounties and arbitrary remote fine-tuning still need stronger privacy, isolation and evaluation infrastructure.

CRITICAL TRUST BOUNDARY

Commitment is not consensus.

The evaluator is named and trusted. SHA-256 binds exact bytes and Ed25519 verifies expected signers, but signatures do not prove training occurred or prevent bad evaluation design.

THE SAFETY BOUNDARY

Admit data, not unknown code.

For this specific model, the evaluator validates numeric low-rank artifacts, architecture and shape, hashes and expected signatures rather than executing arbitrary submitted programs. This does not certify general model safety.

Solana handles
the money rules.

A model evaluator should not pretend a blockchain can judge model quality. Solana is for public escrow state, registered recipients, evaluator-authorized settlement and timeout refunds between unknown counterparties.

IMPLEMENTED VS. LIVE

Native Rust escrow.

The compiled Solana SBF program has passed local LiteSVM tests. The public recorded experiment is unpaid. No finalized funded Devnet training-to-payout transaction is presented, and no custom token exists.

WHY THE BLOCKCHAIN MATTERS

Neutral economic enforcement.

A future customer and independent engineer can agree to locked rules without a custom token. Model files, hidden evaluation data and expensive training stay off-chain. The named evaluator's trust remains visible.
