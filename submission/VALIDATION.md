# Customer validation protocol (not completed interviews)

Status: a proposed research process. No interviews, testimonials, sales, commitments or pricing acceptance are claimed.

Ask a consenting task owner: What specific model failure matters? What is the present baseline? How is success measured independently of training data? Who can license the training and evaluation data? What does the current solution cost? Would outcome-based payment actually reduce risk, given a centralized evaluator and uncompensated losing experiments? What would make them reject this product?

Ask a consenting contributor: What minimum information and expected reward justify entering? How much unpaid compute can they afford? Can they reproduce the evaluation contract? Is winner-take-all acceptable? How would they contest a mistaken rejection?

Record only agreed, attributable facts. An interview log should contain date, permission, role, problem, evidence, objection, next action and whether a paid commitment actually exists. Keep personal contact details and private datasets out of the public repo. Aggregate results without converting "interesting" into "customer".

A first design-partner study should compare total spend and aggregate compute across all contestants against an ordinary fine-tuning contract. Report failures, denominator, elapsed time and evaluator cost. A positive digits benchmark delta does not validate this business hypothesis.

## Proposed study record

Before recruiting, freeze a stronger parent and a properly licensed private evaluation set; separate training, development and evaluation access; define the utility metric, non-regression checks, maximum evaluation cost, contest rules and any artifact reuse rights. Invite consenting known participants and explain evaluator authority, payout/refund limits and uncompensated losing compute. This is a proposed controlled study, not a shipped permissionless market.

Use a private, consented interview log with columns: date, role, problem, present solution, independently supported cost, objection, decision-maker, next action, and explicit paid-commitment evidence. Publish only permissioned aggregates. For each actual study report participating owners/contributors, all submissions, failed experiments, total compute and evaluator spend, winning improvement on the controlled set, total time, dispute/refund events, and any repeat paid use. Record zero where measured and distinguish it from unknown.

Advance only after task owners demonstrate a useful gain at an acceptable total cost and contributors accept the actual risk/reward terms. Otherwise change the task, evaluation design or payment structure before expanding. A future 5% fee remains a hypothesis; it is neither charged by this release nor established by a favorable interview.
